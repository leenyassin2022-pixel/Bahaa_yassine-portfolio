Bahaa Yassine is an Industrial Engineering and Engineering Management student at Beirut Arab University with a strong interest in manufacturing, business operations, and practical engineering applications.

Through his academic studies and hands-on experience, he has developed knowledge and skills related to manufacturing processes, industrial machinery, CNC operations, production environments, technical and graphic design, and business management.

He is particularly interested in understanding how engineering, production, people, technology, and business decisions connect to create efficient operations.

Bahaa approaches projects with curiosity, responsibility, creativity, organization, and a practical problem-solving mindset.

His long-term ambition is to establish and grow his own manufacturing company, combining engineering knowledge, technology, management and practical experience to develop useful and innovative products.

10
Self-Reflection

WHAT CAREER EXPLORATION
CHANGED FOR ME.

The Virtual Career Exploration Project helped me gain a clearer understanding of the career opportunities available to me as an Industrial Engineering and Engineering Management student. Before completing this project, I already had a strong interest in manufacturing and practical engineering, but the different activities allowed me to look at these interests from a broader and more professional perspective.

Through the company observations, I was able to explore different manufacturing environments, including food and beverage production, cosmetics, and pharmaceuticals. Although these industries produce very different products, I noticed that they all depend on similar principles such as efficient processes, quality control, safety, organization, teamwork, and proper use of technology.

Industrial Engineering can be applied across many different industries.

The informational interview was also valuable because it gave me a more realistic understanding of operations and production management. I learned that technical knowledge alone is not enough to succeed in this field. Communication, leadership, problem-solving, time management, and the ability to work effectively with people are equally important.

The project also made me reflect on my own learning style and strengths. As a visual and practical learner, I understand concepts best when I can see how they work and apply them to real situations. This explains why I am particularly interested in manufacturing, machinery, CNC operations, production processes, and other areas where engineering knowledge can be connected directly to practical work.

Overall, this career exploration experience confirmed my interest in combining Industrial Engineering, manufacturing, and management. It also helped me recognize the skills I still need to develop, especially in engineering software, production management, communication, and leadership.

The project gave me greater confidence in my long-term direction.

My goal is not only to work within a manufacturing environment, but eventually to establish and grow my own manufacturing company. I want to use my engineering education, practical experience, management knowledge, and continuous learning to develop useful products, improve processes, and build a successful business.

This project showed me that reaching that goal will require both technical expertise and strong management skills. It has therefore encouraged me to continue learning, gain more practical experience, and develop myself step by step toward becoming both an engineer and a future business leader.
